We are looking for a developer who owns the technical layer of organic search:
site migrations that keep their rankings, page experience, and the structured
data that decides how a page renders in results. You will work in the
templates and the platform, not only in a reporting dashboard, and you will
work directly with content and marketing teams.

## What you'll do

- Own technical SEO on production sites: fix the crawl and index issues
  Google Search Console reports, in the templates themselves.
- Plan and run site migrations without losing organic traffic: URL mapping,
  301 redirects, launch gates and post-launch checks.
- Implement on-page SEO and JSON-LD structured data in Shopify (Liquid),
  WordPress and HubSpot templates.
- Improve page experience: Core Web Vitals and Lighthouse scores, with
  accessible markup to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Work with content and marketing teams on keyword-targeted pages, measure
  the results in GA4, GTM and Google Search Console, and run A/B tests.

## Must-haves

- Hands-on technical SEO and on-page SEO on production sites.
- A site migration delivered with 301 redirects that kept indexed URLs and
  organic traffic.
- Core Web Vitals work with measured before-and-after results.
- JSON-LD structured data.
- Google Search Console, GA4 and GTM day to day.
- CMS depth in at least one of Shopify (Liquid), WordPress or HubSpot, with
  HTML, CSS and JavaScript to implement fixes directly.

## Nice-to-haves

- E-commerce catalog data quality: product titles, descriptions and
  attributes that reach indexable pages clean.
- Python scripting to automate audits and content work.
- Edge-level redirects and caching on Cloudflare.
