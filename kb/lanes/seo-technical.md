We are looking for a developer who owns the technical layer of organic search:
crawlability and indexation, platform migrations that keep their rankings, page
experience, and the structured data that decides how a page renders in results.
You will work in the templates and the server config, not only in a reporting
dashboard, and you will work directly with content and marketing teams.

## What you'll do

- Own crawlability and indexation: robots.txt, XML sitemaps, canonicals,
  index coverage monitoring, and fixing what Search Console reports.
- Plan and execute platform migrations without losing organic traffic: URL
  mapping, 301 redirects, metadata and catalog migration, post-launch
  verification.
- Implement on-page technical SEO in templates: title tags, meta descriptions,
  heading structure, internal linking, and JSON-LD structured data.
- Improve page experience: Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS), PageSpeed and
  Lighthouse scores, image optimization, lazy loading, caching.
- Work with content and marketing teams on keyword-targeted pages, and report
  on what the changes did.

## Must-haves

- Hands-on technical SEO on production sites, in the templates and the
  platform rather than through a plugin alone.
- A platform migration delivered with redirect mapping that preserved indexed
  URLs and organic traffic.
- Core Web Vitals and PageSpeed remediation with measured before-and-after
  results.
- Structured data (JSON-LD), title tags, meta descriptions, canonicals,
  robots.txt and XML sitemaps.
- Google Search Console and Analytics day to day, including index coverage and
  performance reports.
- CMS depth in at least one of Shopify (Liquid), WordPress or HubSpot CMS, with
  HTML, CSS and JavaScript to implement fixes directly.

## Nice-to-haves

- E-commerce catalog data quality: product titles, descriptions and attributes
  that reach indexable pages clean.
- Google Tag Manager, log-file or crawl analysis, and content scoring against
  target keywords.
- Scripting to automate audits and content operations (Python, Node.js).
- Self-managed hosting experience (Linux, Apache or Nginx) for server-level
  fixes such as redirects and caching.
