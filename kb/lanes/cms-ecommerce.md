We need a CMS and e-commerce developer who takes client sites from design to
launch: custom themes, editor-friendly templates, storefront features, and the
integrations that connect content, catalog, and CRM.

## What you'll do

- Build and customize Shopify storefronts: Liquid theme development, custom
  sections, product catalog and checkout flows, app and API integrations.
- Develop HubSpot CMS sites: HubL modules and page templates, editor-
  configurable components, CRM configuration and handoff.
- Deliver WordPress builds (Elementor) and headless CMS work with Contentful,
  including content modeling and migration.
- Own projects end to end as the client-facing developer: scoping, QA,
  launches, redirects, and post-launch support.
- Keep sites fast and findable: on-page SEO, Core Web Vitals, PageSpeed
  optimization, image optimization, lazy loading, caching.

## Must-haves

- Shopify (Liquid, custom themes) and at least one of HubSpot CMS (HubL) or
  WordPress delivered for real clients.
- Headless CMS experience (Contentful) — certification a strong plus.
- JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, React, HTML5, CSS3; REST APIs for
  integrations.
- E-commerce fundamentals: product catalogs, inventory data, migrations,
  redirects, launch checklists.
- Direct client communication and independent delivery.

## Nice-to-haves

- Next.js or Node.js backend work behind CMS front ends.
- Technical SEO: structured data (JSON-LD), Core Web Vitals remediation.
- Git-based theme workflows and CI (GitHub Actions).
